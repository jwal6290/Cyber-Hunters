KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Common Types of Scams

Explore some of the most common ways scammers attempt to deceive people.

01

Phishing Scams

Fraudulent emails or messages designed to steal personal information, passwords, or financial details.

02

Text Message Scams

Suspicious text messages that may pretend to come from banks, delivery services, or government agencies.

03

Fake Websites

Websites created to imitate legitimate businesses and trick visitors into sharing sensitive information.

04

Prize and Giveaway Scams

Messages claiming you have won a prize but requiring payment or personal information to claim it.

05

Romance Scams

Scammers create fake online relationships to build trust and eventually request money or financial assistance.

06

Job Scams

Fake employment opportunities that may request upfront payments or sensitive personal information.

SPOT THE RED FLAGS

Warning Signs of a Scam

Be cautious when a message includes any of these common warning signs.

Urgent language: Messages pressuring you to act immediately.
Unexpected links: Links asking you to log in or provide personal information.
Requests for payment: Unexpected demands for money, gift cards, or cryptocurrency.
Suspicious senders: Unfamiliar email addresses, phone numbers, or accounts.
Too good to be true: Offers promising unrealistic rewards or guaranteed results.
PROTECT YOURSELF

Simple Ways to Stay Safe

Build safer online habits with these simple precautions.

Verify Before You Click

Check website addresses and sender information before opening links or attachments.

Protect Your Information

Never share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive financial information with unknown sources.

Take Your Time

Do not let unexpected messages pressure you into making rushed decisions.
